# Insurance-Risk-Claim-Analysis-Power-BI-


Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis

A Power BI dashboard developed to analyze insurance policies, claims, customer demographics, vehicle details, and risk patterns.

Project Overview :

The dashboard provides a view of key insurance metrics and helps identify patterns in claim amounts.

Key KPIs :
Total Policies: 37,542
Total Claim Amount: $187.8M
Average Claim Frequency: 0.5
Average Claim Amount: $5.0K

Analysis Covered:
Claim Amount by Car Use
Claim Amount by Car Make
Claim Amount by Coverage Zone
Claim Amount by Age Group
Claim Amount by Car Year
Claim Amount by Kids Driving
Claim Amount by Education
Claim Amount by Education & Marital Status
Policy distribution by Gender

Tools Used:

Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Cleaning | Data Visualization

Key Insights: 
Private vehicles contribute the highest claim amount.
Claim amounts vary across different car manufacturers.
Coverage zones show different levels of claim exposure.
Age groups have different claim patterns.
Education and marital status provide useful customer segmentation.
Male and female policy counts are almost evenly distributed.
